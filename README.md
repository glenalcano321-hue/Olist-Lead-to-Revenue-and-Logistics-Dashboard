Olist Funnel & Logistics Dashboards

Two interactive Excel dashboards built on Olist's public Brazilian e-commerce data. One follows the revenue side of the business, from first lead to seller sales. The other covers operations: delivery, freight cost and inventory.

Lead-to-Revenue Funnel
Question: How well does Olist turn marketing leads into sellers who actually sell, and which channels and reps drive that revenue?

8,000 leads to 842 signed sellers (10.5% conversion), taking 48 days on average to close
Only 45% of signed sellers made a sale (380 sellers, R$677K in sales)
Paid and organic search convert about 4x better than email (12.3% and 11.8% vs 3.0%)
Joins the marketing funnel to 112K order lines by seller ID. All charts share one set of slicers (filters).

Logistics Performance Review
Question: What should we fix first to reach 95% on-time delivery and cut shipping cost?

Delivery: 93.2% of orders arrived on time (target 95%), taking 12.6 days on average. When a seller hands an order to the carrier late, it arrives late 21% of the time, vs 5% when they hand it over on time. The Northeast has the most late deliveries (12.7%).
Freight: Shipping costs 16.6% of sale (target 15%), or R$2.25M in total. Shipping between regions costs about twice as much per kg as shipping within a state (R$8.90 vs R$4.54).
Inventory: In an ABC analysis, 26% of products bring in 80% of revenue**. The sheet also compares two forecasting methods (moving average vs exponential smoothing) and includes a safety stock and reorder point calculator.
Covers 99,441 orders (2016–2018). Includes a KPI reference with formulas and targets, a glossary, and data quality notes.

Built with: Excel (pivot tables, slicers, formulas that update live). Data is the public Olist dataset from Kaggle.
